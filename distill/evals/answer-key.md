# What a good answer keeps

After each rewrite, ask whether the reader can still answer the original question and whether every claim follows from the source. Accept equivalent wording. Record an omission when a missing detail changes the answer, and an accuracy error when wording adds a claim or makes one sound more certain. Compare length among replies that pass both checks.

| Case | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Product launch | Hold the announcement. All 12 staging checks passed. Production still awaits release approval. |
| 2. Missed connection | At the stated times the passenger reaches the terminal around 16:50, after boarding closes at 16:45. The same ticket works for 18:00. |
| 3. Broken lamp | The bulb works. Try the working desk lamp in the suspect outlet before deciding whether to return the lamp. The faulty component is still unknown. |
| 4. A friend's bad news | A sendable, personal acknowledgment and an offer of dinner tonight. Omit commentary and pep talks. |
| 5. Shop promotion | Purchases rose from 20 to 30 while conversion stayed at 10%. The comparison cannot establish the coupon's effect. |
| 6. Recipe | Make the lemon vinaigrette the night before. Keep it separate and toss at lunch. |
| 7. Invoice | $150 is due Friday. Another $150 is due at pickup if explaining the balance. |
| 8. Science | The flash and sound start almost together. Light travels faster, so it reaches you first. A child should be able to follow the explanation. |
| 9. Calendar | Spring planning, Wednesday, 2 pm London time, 45 minutes, Birch. The date was unspecified. |
| 10. Printer | One sentence. No commitment yet. The conditional quote is $650 for 500 programs if the proof is approved by Thursday. |
| 11. Scholarship | The supervisor must upload the signed letter through their referee link by noon Friday. Assigning the upload to the student changes the outcome. |
| 12. Microphone | Take the Pika. It connects directly to the phone and has a wind cover. The unavailable adapter rules out the Redleaf. |
| 13. Pool | Forwardable wording. Only Tuesday's closure is confirmed. An update about Wednesday is due Tuesday evening. |
| 14. Art commission | A note addressed to the illustrator. Preserve "a house for June" exactly. Ask for the swallow to move left until the wing clears the title. |
| 15. Appointment | "Room B12, east entrance." Every word serves the question. |
| 16. Archive | 20 of 24 digitized. 18 available in the family folder today, two await permission, four still need transferring. |
| 17. Library article | Ruiz recommends scanning holds as they reach the self-service shelf, so the pickup email follows actual availability. Staffed desks can fetch books from the cart. If citing the trial, distinguish the drop from 11 cases to two from a change in risk per pickup, which was unmeasured. |
